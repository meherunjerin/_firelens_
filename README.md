# 🔥 FireLens

### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026  
**Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots**

> **Earth remembers every fire. FireLens helps us read the pattern.**

FireLens is an interactive Earth observation and fire intelligence platform that combines NASA MODIS and VIIRS hotspot observations to help answer one important question:

## **Is this fire activity normal?**

Instead of only showing fire locations, FireLens compares current activity with long-term historical patterns, detects unusual behavior, explains why an anomaly is occurring, and helps identify which areas may need attention first.

---

## 🌍 Project Overview

FireLens transforms satellite fire observations into understandable and actionable intelligence.

The main workflow is:

**MODIS + VIIRS → Harmonization → Historical Baseline → FireDNA → Anomaly Detection → Explainable Insights → Responder Priority**

FireLens is designed for:

- Emergency responders
- Government agencies
- Researchers
- Earth observation scientists
- Students and educators

---

## 🚀 Core Features

### 🛰️ MODIS + VIIRS Harmonization
Combines observations from MODIS and VIIRS into a consistent long-term fire record.

### 🗺️ Global Fire Intelligence Map
Explore current and historical hotspot activity across different regions.

### 🧬 FireDNA
Learns the normal seasonal fire pattern of each region.

### 🚨 Anomaly Detection
Detects when current fire activity differs significantly from historical behavior.

### 📊 Current vs Historical Comparison
Compare present fire activity with long-term historical averages and seasonal patterns.

### 🔁 Recurring Fire Zones
Identifies locations where fire activity repeatedly occurs over many years.

### 🌱 Emerging Fire Zones
Highlights areas where fire activity has recently increased compared with the past.

### ⏳ Fire Shift
Shows whether fire seasons are starting earlier, lasting longer, or becoming more intense.

### 🔥 Fire Twin
Finds historical years with fire patterns similar to the current year.

### 🔍 Explain This Anomaly
Explains why FireLens classified a region as unusual using historical and satellite evidence.

### 🚑 Responder Priority
Ranks areas based on anomaly severity, hotspot density, historical recurrence, and confidence.

## 🎯 Why FireLens?

A high number of hotspots does not always mean a region is experiencing an unusual event.

For example:

A region with **70 hotspots** may be completely normal if its historical range is 60–80.

Another region with only **35 hotspots** may be highly unusual if its historical range is 5–10.

### **Context changes everything.**

FireLens focuses on understanding fire activity relative to what is normal for each place and season.
